Direction labels describe image motion, not legal road direction. A warning means a tracked object moved opposite to your selected image direction. Moving-camera perspective, curves, intersections and parallax can cause false alarms. Do not treat this as proof of wrong-way driving.

    What this version can and cannot do

    ✓ Live rear-camera video; vehicle and traffic-light object detection; approximate object tracking; GPS speed/heading when available; audible experimental alerts; local event export.
    ⚠ Signal color is estimated experimentally and can be wrong due to distance, glare or other lights. ✗ It does not reliably interpret traffic rules, one-way restrictions, Google Maps navigation or reliable wrong-way violations. It does not record video or upload camera frames. Object-detection models are downloaded from external CDNs.
